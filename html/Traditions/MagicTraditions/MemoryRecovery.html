Memory Recovery

The process of recovering memories from behind a partition.

Explanation:

Standard practice is to start at the earliest memory lost, chronologically speaking. However, other potential starting points, known as 'points of clarity', can also serve as a starting point. The idea is to find a memory with enough connections outside of the partition that there isn't as much to recover.

Memory recovery often results in involuntary muscle relaxation, which means finding a stable seated position is very important.
