03/18/1980: Auror Department Memo

Auror Department meeting on 03/19/1980 at 6:30 AM. Aurors use Auror Deparment private Floo to travel, required to sign up for staggered arrival.

Vanguard members will come to the Ministry during the meeting, will be kept in cell waiting area with Dawlish, Ibex, and Savage.

Press conference tomorrow at 7:00 AM, Vanguard members can attend. Aurors will provide double the usual security, using Order members to fill in the gaps. Order members will be assigned an Auror partner.

General lecture about treating Regulus with respect.
