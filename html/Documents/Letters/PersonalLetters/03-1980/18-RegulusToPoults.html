18 - Regulus to Poults

Summary:

Regulus reassures Poults that she has nothing to apologize for.

Regulus tells Poults about the press conference. He acknowledges that this will spawn questions about what happened during his incarceration, then asks if there's anything she would like him to include. He says he plans to talk to her in person before the press conference.

Regulus compliments Poults' leadership, saying that she is the 'most qualified individual who could have possibly succeeded Mr. Crouch'.
