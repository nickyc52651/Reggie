18 - Regulus to Brown

Summary:

Regulus didn't encourage Mr. Brown's children to leave Hogwarts during Christmas break, but he doesn't disagree with the decision.

Mr. Brown wasn't consulted about the match between Almira and Thomas Selwyn. Regulus says that he's happy to separate them if Almira objects to the match, but otherwise, he's hesitant to do so. He references his relationship with Vera to explain his reasoning.

There is a place in pureblood society for Mr. Brown and his family.
