18 - Regulus to Bagnold

Summary:

Regulus is responding to a letter from Minister Bagnold. He starts by thanking her for her 'kind words' and saying that he forgives her for whatever actions she presumably apologized for in her letter.

Regulus says that, due to the dwindling power of pureblood society, their resources are 'being subjected to the same fate'. He offers a quid pro quo, whereby Minister Bagnold would receive pureblood funding for the Ministry dependent on her promising seats in the Wizengamot to the following people:

  • Remus - or Sirius, depending on who's available
  • Lord Henry Abbott
  • Frank Longbottom - potential conflict of interest due to his position as an Auror
  • Gabrial Macmillan
  • Gideon Prewett
  • William Weasley - his spot will be taken by his mom until he's of age

Regulus re-emphasizes that he doesn't expect any of the above to be involved in his own trial. He wants them to be part of the laws that are being passed post-war.

Regulus hopes that Vanguard members will be given their Wizengamot seats after their house arrests. He proposes a 1-year trial, to be made permanent if the other Wizengamot members approve.

Regulus asks what to do about the Exiled lords, mentioning Arthur Greengrass specifically. He suggests an in-person discussion about the matter.
