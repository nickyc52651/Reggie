18 - Regulus to Scott

Summary:

Oliver Corbins, who has been living with Scott, is asking questions about his father's Wizengamot case. The Auror Department has some of Corbins' memory vials.

Davidson invited members of The Dagger's team to come to Lestrange's parties. Corbins accepted, but Regulus doesn't know if he won or participated in auctions. He must have had appointments with Pet, but Regulus doesn't remember anything. Corbins didn't mention inviting Oliver to Lestrange's parties.

Regulus compliments Oliver Corbins profusely, stating that he flies better than his father ever did.

There is a place for the Scotts in pureblood society.
