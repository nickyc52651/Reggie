18 - Regulus to Lucretia (unsent)

Summary:

Regulus laments the death of Fabian Prewett, saying that if he could, he would die in his place.

Regulus apologizes for the distance Orion forced on Lucretia. Apparently, she sent letters to Grimmauld Place, and he prevented them from reaching Regulus and Sirius. Regulus would be open to starting a new relationship with Lucretia, but wants her to meet with Narcissa first.

Regulus tries to explain why he wants Lucretia to meet with Narcissa, but after listing how many people have betrayed him, he abandons the point of the letter. He ends with the following two lines:

I miss you, James.

I'm sorry I wasn't good enough for you to stay.
