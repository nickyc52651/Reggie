18 - Regulus to Travis

Summary:

Regulus offers his condolences about the death of Travis' friend and offers to fund a trip to America, whether that stay is temporary or permanent.

Regulus says if Travis wants to go back to being an American Auror, he will write recommendation letters. He then amends the statement to say that he'll have James write them.

Regulus says he would love to see Travis, but he understands if Travis wants to keep his distance.

Regulus tells Travis about his planned press conference and apologizes for planning it.
