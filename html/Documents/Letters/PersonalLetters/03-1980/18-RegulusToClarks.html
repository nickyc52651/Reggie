18 - Regulus to Clarks

Summary:

Regulus says Katie and Archibald do not need to apologize for their father's actions. He promises to talk to Skeeter and asks them to forward him any additional letters she sends them.

Regulus promises to write to Wal about letting both Katie and Archibald stay at Macnair Manor, since they presumably lost their home due to their father's incarceration.

Regulus implicitly encourages both recipients to continue the Empire, telling them where to find the garden and the third floor passageway, and to reach out to Barty and James for ideas.

He more pointedly tells them to reach out to Remus and Slughorn for support.
