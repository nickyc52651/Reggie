18 - Regulus to David

Summary:

Regulus apologizes for David and Andrei's inability to go back to Russia, and offers to find them somewhere to live.

Regulus admits that he wants David to stay in London so that he can go into business with Severus Snape, and offers to invest in the business. He's hoping that this potential shop could provide healing potions for Sanctuary, and says that Andrei's knowledge of dark magic could help with long-tem effects of The Artist's curses.

Regulus tells David about the press conference and apologizes, saying, 'I am sorry that I cannot keep your name safe from my reputation'.
