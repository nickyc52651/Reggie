18 - Regulus to Slughorn

Summary:

Regulus refuses Slughorn's implied offer of potions supplies.

Regulus explains that David likely refused Slughorn's offer of mentorship because of trauma from Karkaroff. Regulus says he will talk to David about changing his mind, but makes no promises.

Regulus claims he doesn't know how often the Empire was stealing supplies from Slughorn, but offers to reimburse him based on Slughorn's records (it is likely that none exist).

Regulus says that an organization like the Empire shouldn't be necessary moving forward, but he'll help anonymously if it becomes necessary.
