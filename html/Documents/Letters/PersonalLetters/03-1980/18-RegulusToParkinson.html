18 - Regulus to Lord Parkinson

Summary:

Regulus gives reassurances about his health and the future of his House.

Regulus says he'll talk about the concerns raised in the previous letter, but redirects future concerns to Lucius.

Regulus asks to be addressed 'as an ally', i.e. by his first name. He asks for the same thing in reverse.

Regulus says that Lady Parkinson's connection to Lestrange Manor remain unspoken. He also says that if Baby Parkinson's paternity is called into question, she'll be kicked out of pureblood society
