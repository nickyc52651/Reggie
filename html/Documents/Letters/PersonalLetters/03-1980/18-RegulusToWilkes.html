18 - Regulus to Lord Wilkes

Summary:

Regulus gives reassurances about his health and the future of his House.

Regulus says he'll talk about the concerns raised in the previous letter, but redirects future concerns to Evan.

Regulus asks to be addressed 'as an ally', i.e. by his first name. He asks for the same thing in reverse.

Regulus apologizes for Ian Wilkes' death, saying that there was no excuse for it and that he did not need to die.
