03/18/1980: Pt. 3 Aftermath

Summary:

Regulus is clearly not well, very dissociated and not thinking clearly, losing track of the year. He also can't hear Lucius' words clearly, meaning his hearing is going in and out. Kingsley suggests stopping for the day. Lucius refuses, saying Regulus just needs a glass of water. Frank says Regulus needs a glass of water, a walk, and a moment to breathe. Lucius offers Rousseau a drink, but Rousseau redirects to Regulus, who isn't talking. Rousseau prompts Regulus for an audible response, but doesn't receive one.

David knocks on the table, recognizing that Pet is here now, and that Lestrange Manor communication will be the best way to get through to Regulus. Frank ushers Kingsley and Proudfoot out of the room to allow Pet and David to talk. Pet thinks this is an appointment. David comforts Pet in every way he knows how while Moody keeps the others at bay. Pet is confused, because Moody isn't a client, so it isn't sure why he's watching it. David tried to tell Pet that Moody is safe, but he fails. Pet insists that it isn't safe. David agrees.

David asks Moody if he listens to Pet, using Regulus' summary of Rodolphus coming to Slughorn's party as an example. He points out that Shacklebolt accused Pet of helping Master find a new pet, when all Pet wants to do is protect others from Master. David says that all Pet wanted to do during Slughorn's party was protect students from Master, but he couldn't because that would reveal him as Pet. Moody almost tells David about the press conference, but Lucius stops him.

David insists that, for the next portion of the interview, Pet be allowed to stand and walk while answering questions. Moody agrees.
