03/18/1980: Regulus and Frank Talk about Lucretia

Summary:

Regulus is editing the letter to Lucretia (see side menu), but stops. He asks Frank if he knows Lucretia, and Frank responds that he does. He felt that Lucretia hated Orion, but felt sorry for Regulus and Sirius.

Regulus summarizes Lucretia's letter to him. He says he's not sure if Lucretia is approaching him as a pureblood, or as his aunt, or if she wants something, etc. He rejects the possibility that Lucretia could just want to be his aunt, because he can't understand why anyone would want to be connected to him. This leads into Regulus talking about the negative consequences that could come to James as a result of being connected to Regulus.

Regulus tells Frank about the press conference and explains his reasoning. He spirals into an anxiety attack about failing in his duties as Lord Black. He says he needs a break (he wants a calming draught), and after being reminded that he's a vegetarian, he accepts the offer of a vegetarian sandwich for lunch.
