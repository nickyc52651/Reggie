03/18/1980: David Comes to Grimmauld

Summary:

Shacklebolt introduces David as a member of the Victim Advocacy Team, then explains the reasons why he was hired. David explains that Regulus is getting in the way of Pet getting the help he needs. He then hands Regulus a drawing Lindsey made for Pet.

Regulus asks about Phoebe, then directly asks David why he's here. David explains that Pet doesn't talk, not even to help himself, so David is there to speak for Pet. After further prompting, David admits that Rousseau doesn't want him talking to Pet, listing off a few syptoms. He explains that he's talking to Regulus because he's not allowed to talk to Pet. He also says he wants to listen to the interview, defending Regulus and placing blame solely on Rodolphus. Regulus refuses, wanting to defend David against issues from defending him.

Regulus abruptly changes the topic, instructing Frank to hand David the letters he'd written to David and the other Lestrange Manor Silenced.

Before leaving, Regulus offers David the chance to stay overnight at Grimmauld Place so they can talk.
