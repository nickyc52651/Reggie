03/18/1980: Dagger Interview Pt. 3

Summary:

Shacklebolt asks about anything else Regulus remembers about the day the Unforgivable policy was announced. Regulus talks about the confrontation with Mulciber in the library. Then, moving on, he talks about Slughorn's end of year party, admitting that Rodolphus asked him for help meeting underage students, and he complied.

Regulus denies knowing that Vera was a half-blood, saying that he would have protected Vera from his father. Regulus remembers the observed training session on the day of his birthday gala, then briefly summarizes Voldemort's visit to Grimmauld Place.

Regulus summarizes Pet's debut, but starts to struggle when he admits that he wanted a numbing potion. Frank interrupts, imploring Kingsley to listen to Regulus and asking what he could have done to prevent Pet's debut. Kingsley concedes the point.

Regulus talks about the initiation ceremony, admitting that he carved the word into the Muggle's chest and raped McKinney (it's implied that he remembers this). Regulus summarizes the initiation duels, then the Cruciatus trial. Kingsley likens Regulus' strength of will to Sirius', which leads Regulus into a spiral about not being Sirius' brother. Kingsley instructs Proudfoot to leave that out of the transcript.

Kingsley asks about Phoebe and Fred. Regulus admits that Phoebe and Fred were brought to Grimmauld Place as a result of a bargain he made to prevent Tom from being branded. He admits that he was worried that Rodolphus would ask him for sexual favors before he would send adults instead of children, and Regulus couldn't comply with that. Kingsley asks if Regulus would have freed Phoebe and Fred, given the chance. Regulus admits that he doesn't know, which Kingsley takes as a no.

Kingsley asks if Regulus knew that Order members had snuck into the initiation ball. Regulus says he did, and that he thought it was a stupid decision. Kingsley says that the Order members in question were worried about Regulus, then pivots to Evan's concern about Regulus' growing dependence on numbing potions. Regulus deflects, saying he needed them because of Greyback, and that Rodolphus wouldn't give him healing elixirs. Kingsley asks why Regulus didn't ask Karkaroff for the recipe. Regulus admits that Rodolphus sold Pet to Mulciber at that ball, saying that any other answer risks breaking the Fidelius.

Kingsley asks if Regulus had any idea that he wouldn't be coming home from the Minister's speech. Regulus says he didn't, and that he didn't fight back because the room was spinning. This draws Kingsley's attention, implying that the Auror Department didn't know Regulus had been drugged. Kingsley seems shocked that Regulus doesn't remember anything else about the attack.

Kingsley says he's been instructed to ask very broad questions about Lestrange Manor, then allows Regulus to summarize his time at Lestrange Manor, requiring Kingsley's prompting to continue at times. Kingsley understands, through the summary, that Rodolphus was encouraging Regulus' dependence on potions and manipulating his memories. Regulus briefly mentions the Named meeting where he was high on buzzing potion, then writing the July letter to Dumbledore. Kingsley asks about the anniversary dinner, and Regulus gives a sparse summary.

Kingsley asks if Regulus saw Pet at Lestrange Manor. Regulus admits that he wasn't thinking clearly, but he paid attention to the clients. He starts to explain his mindset at Lestrange Manor, saying that he thought Lindsey was part Veela at first. He talks about Lindsey, Travis, David, and Andrei, then cuts himself off. He lists a number of mundane details about Lestrange Manor.

Kingsley directly asks Regulus about Rodolphus' claims that he was present at all of Rodolphus' parties. Regulus denies being a client, but says that he couldn't leave the manor, so he was there. He then states that Rodolphus never invited him to a party, and that he enjoyed reminding Regulus that he couldn't do anything to stop him. Regulus then admits going to the parties because he wanted potions. The following conversation ensues.

KINGSLEY: You attended the parties, and did nothing to stop them, and you did it because you wanted potions?

REGULUS: ... I... Yes. I don't... That sounds...

KINGSLEY: It sounds despicable. Because it is.

REGULUS: ... I think he broke me.

KINGSLEY: Maybe. But he shattered Pet.

REGULUS: ... I know.

Kingsley confronts Regulus about his use of the word 'inconvenience' to describe William Snowbell's death. Regulus says that if he'd allowed himself to care about Snowbell's death, he would have drowned in grief. Essentially, he was avoiding feelings. Kingsley asks about the techniques Regulus developed, leading to an in-depth explanation of training for The Dagger's team. Regulus turns it into a bit of a tirade, after which Kingsley redirects to Snowbell. Regulus' response is, "And damn Snowbell for not inventing a solution to broom transportation before he was unworthy enough to be killed by The General," thus demonstrating how removed from his emotions he was as The Dagger.

Kingsley asks about the Inferi. Regulus, still angry on the back of the broom technique tirade, gives a rant where he outlines how to control an Inferius. His explanation reveals an in-depth knowledge of dark magic. Kingsley says he put in a lot of effort for a cause he claims to hate, and Regulus clarifies that he doesn't hate the cause - he hated that the war put his friends in danger.

Kingsley asks about the murder of the Joneses, trying to see if Regulus verified the accusations against any of his victims. Regulus admits he didn't.

Kingsley reveals that he has Greyback's necklace (Rodolphus' copy), and that it was given to the Auror Department when he was arrested. He verifies that this is the necklace Regulus gave Greyback (Regulus apparently doesn't remember the difference), causing a spiral about making the deal with Greyback. Regulus admits that he offered to let Greyback turn him, which shocks Kingsley. Once Regulus' spiral ends (when he reaches the celebration after the deal), Kingsley touches the necklace to see if it still works. Regulus confirms it does, seemingly desperate to get Kingsley to stop. He clarifies that the necklace has to be touched with one's bare hand to activate, because Protean charms are based on heat.

Regulus asks why Kingsley cares about the deal with Greyback. Kingsley, now more compassionate, says that he wants to show the other side of the coin. Then he asks about Regulus' potion use, and the pressure Greyback put on him. Regulus tries to avoid it, then, when directly confronted with his avoidance, he spirals. The spiral ends when Regulus realizes that he was using 'depulso' to mount his broom back then - not 'impulsum'.

Kingsley asks about Orion's campaign for Minister, then about the tension between Cygnus and Orion, and about the responses of the other Named to Orion's campaign. When he gets to Greyback, Regulus stumbles into what sounds like anti-werewolf sentiment, but is actually anger about what Greyback did to Remus. Regulus conceals this fact from Kingsley, begging him to stop asking questions about Greyback. Kingsley complies.

Regulus summarizes his meeting with Peter as The Dagger. He narrowly avoids admitting that the Marauders are Animagi, focusing on Pettigrew's despicable behavior, specifically his shock that Voldemort wasn't going to kill Remus for being a werewolf. Regulus then admits to trying to warn Dumbledore in his letter. He says he didn't reach out to James or Sirius because he didn't think it was safe, so he couldn't make himself reach out.

Regulus talks about the initiation test. When asked if there's anything else important, Regulus says there isn't, but Lucius corrects him, saying that he was tortured for the death of Caleb Bell, which occurred during the initiation test. Regulus doesn't know who killed him.

Kingsley asks Regulus why he founded Vanguard, and Regulus admits it was because of his conversation with Tom about nukes. Kingsley stops the transcript to ask if that's why Regulus is so focused on defending the Statute of Secrecy, and Regulus admits that it is. Regulus also admits that Vanguard was originally a suicide mission.

Regulus briefly mentions recruiting Thomas and Wal to Vanguard, then pivots to the fallout from The General's death. Kingsley clarifies that there was an increase in memory abuse from Rodolphus and sexual abuse from Greyback, and that Regulus coped with it using potions. Then he asks if Regulus' stay at Macnair Manor was a result of that pressure. Regulus starts on a tangent that doesn't make sense, whereupon Kingsley declares a break.
