03/18/1980: Frank Helps Post-Nightmare

Summary:

Regulus left lunch to take a nap (take a potion), then actually fell asleep.

Regulus wakes up from recovering the pet memory (see above) and realizes that he must have been thinking about James. He regresses to Pet.

Pet looks for the mirror to cut itself, and, unable to find it, curls up on the ground. Frank knocks several times, eventually convincing Pet to open the door (it doesn't want to hide). He realizes he's dealing with Pet almost immediately.

Pet scratches itself in a way that concerns Frank. Frank grabs its wrists to stop the damage, then realizes that it's crying. Frank reveals that he's seen some of the memory vial memories.

Frank offers Pet a blanket or shirt to cover itself. He doesn't want to let go of Pet's wrists and risk it hurting itself again, so he pulls the bedcovers off and only lets go of Pet's wrists to wrap them around its shoulders.

Frank announces that he's sending James a Patronus. Pet blurts out 'no', then tries to cover its head with its arms to avoid the torture gifts for talking. Frank tries a different approach, asking Pet if it wants him to tell James about the press conference. Pet refuses.

Frank, in an effort to show Pet that talking is allowed, starts talking about James. He says he tried to get James to become an Auror, but James refused. He says that Sirius wanted to be an Auror. After going on for a bit, he prompts Pet to talk by asking it if it believes it can trust James. Pet says it doesn't know if James is real. Frank points out that Polyjuice Potion can't copy jewelry, and that if Pet sees James' ring, it will know that he's real.

Frank talks about being there when Rodolphus was given the Dementor's Kiss. He says that Evan and Barty were both there.

Frank explains, in long form, what the war (and by extension, Regulus) looked like to him. He touches on the Order meeting that Regulus crashed, the Lestrange Manor mission, and the Fake Initiation, and several other things. He starts crying when he gets to Regulus' arrest. He reveals that he almost quit his job after that.

Frank's monologue succeeds in bringing Regulus back.

Regulus responds to Frank's monologue by explaining why he thinks he's not a good person, using his wish for Snape to fail his initiation as an example. He justifies his defense of Sirius' pronouns by saying, "I'm a pureblood. We're particular about names."

Regulus confesses that he doesn't remember raping children, but acknowledges that that doesn't mean he didn't do it. He remembers being forced to rape Percy.

Frank confesses that he can't fight the Imperius, using that to explain why Regulus isn't responsible for all of his actions during the war.

Frank explains that fighting the Imperius once is like lifting your one-rep max, meaning that you can't do it many times in quick succession. He also emphasizes that it's harder to fight the Imperius when you're already under stress.

Frank briefly summarizes Cooking with Kreacher, mentioning off-hand that he didn't know what the nightmares sounded like until now.

Apparently, Pet is very vocal when recovering memories, essentially saying everything it couldn't when the events were happening.

Regulus thanks Frank for helping him come back to himself, and they stand up, starting to prepare for the interview.
