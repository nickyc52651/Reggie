03/17/1980: Cooking with Kreacher

Summary:

After Rousseau leaves, Regulus spends several minutes staring into the fireplace he just left through. He walks into the kitchen, where Kreacher is preparing something for dinner. Kreacher calls Regulus 'Master' and tells him to chop cucumbers for the salad. He then reveals that he has taken Regulus' potions knife and put it in the potions room at Black Manor.

Regulus asks Frank to help him get past a blood ward to access a dagger hidden in the wall. Frank initially refuses, then agrees to help. In the process, he remembers that there was a blood ward on the door to the Lestrange Manor dungeons. Regulus confirms that the ward was keyed to him, then starts cutting the cucumber with the knife from the panel. He's blurry now, which means he accidentally cuts himself.

Kreacher claims that he shattered all of Regulus' healing potions, so Regulus fashions a bandage out of his shirt. To do this, he has to take off his shirt, which reveals his scars to Frank.

Regulus, overwhelmed, reverts to Pet.

Kreacher admits to Pet that he wasn't able to break all of the healing potions. He then tries to guide it towards the dining room, but fails because Pet is intent on kneeling and not moving. Frank recognizes this and explains to Kreacher. Kreacher comes up with the solution that Pet will need to pretend to be The Dagger. He further plays into the facade by claiming that Pet needs to go to its room to wait for Greyback.

In Regulus' room, a dissociated Regulus insists that no amount of protection will keep it safe from Greyback. It asks Frank to apologize to James for it, asking if it's not good enough for James to come back. It suggests letting James 'have' it as a way to gain his favor again, even suggesting potions as a way to make the process easier for itself.

Frank says he's going to stay in Regulus' room for the night, along with his wife. Pet thinks that's because they're both clients. Frank, in an effort to prove that's not the case, talks about why he became an Auror. There are a few tangents, but eventually Frank settles on explaining his view of what happened during Regulus' incarceration, which is based entirely on Thomas' testimony and can be summarized as follows:

  • There was a near-constant problem of inmates getting into fights
  • Walden tended to get in the way of thses fights, particularly at night
    • This was good, because the fights were worse at night
  • None of the Aurors on duty bothered to try and stop the fights
  • Sirius, Theodore, and Thomas were ordered to stay out of it
  • Regulus wasn't eating much (implied slight against the Auror Department)

Frank realizes that Pet wasn't allowed to laugh at Lestrange Manor, which prompts him to hug Pet. Alice enters and, after empathizing with Frank for a moment, convinces Frank to leave Pet alone so it can sleep in private.

Pet goes to sleep and has a nightmare (see Pet's memories). When Regulus wakes up, he leaves his bedroom to get potions.
