09/08/1972: The Empire Is Founded

Summary:

Regulus returns to the dorm after Slughorn's party and talks to Evan about his plans for Peter Pettigrew. Evan informs Regulus that Thomas Blair knows (via Thomas Blair's Sister) of a third floor passageway to Hogsmeade and is willing to share it. Regulus says he'll ask Barty to set up a meeting with Blair

Technically the founding of The Empire.
