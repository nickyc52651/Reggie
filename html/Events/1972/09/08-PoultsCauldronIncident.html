09/08/1972: Poults Cauldron Incident

Summary:

Students are learning how to brew a simple Healing Draught

Barty attempts to introduce himself to Slughorn, but is interrupted by Joseph Poults and Forest Ibex asking about the Slytherin Cauldron Frogs.

Regulus grinds some Lacewing Flies into powder, and Evan puts them into Poults' cauldron, destabilizing his potion and making the cauldron explode. Slughorn takes 10 points from each Gryffindor for the incident.
