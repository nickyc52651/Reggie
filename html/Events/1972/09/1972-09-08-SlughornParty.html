09/08/1972: Slughorn Party

Summary:

Ian Wilkes isn't there due to his dosing the day before.

Regulus meets Narcissa so they can walk down together; Narcissa suggests that he start an alliance with Wulfric Mulciber and keep an eye on Dawn Avery as a potential future wife. She approves of Regulus' alliance with Severus Snape as a way to create distance between him and Sirius.

Narcissa suggests using the Come and Go Room to allow the Black brothers to have private conversations.

When they reach the party, they go over to Lucius, who introduces Regulus to Thorfinn Rowle. They discuss Lord Rowle's career and recent initiative ( Ministry Blood Status Tests).

Lucius introduces Regulus to Theodore Nott, Caspar Avery, and Thomas Selwyn - they discuss why Ian Wilkes isn't there and how Wulfric Mulciber will likely look for revenge. Regulus offers himself as a potential ally in getting revenge.

Theodore offers himself as an ally for Regulus, but is rebuffed because he calls Regulus the 'true heir'. Regulus uses Theodore's past relationship with Narcissa to insult him.

Regulus asks Theodore about Snape's annoyance with James' crush on Lily and receives an explanation, then implicitly admits to the Poults Cauldron Incident.

Bellatrix arrives late because she was writing to her fiance. She asks Regulus how he's been, but is interrupted by Narcissa.

Mulciber and Regulus agree to target Lily for revenge, using her friendship with Snape (and frequent dungeon visits) as an access point (Pettigrew Hair Incident).
