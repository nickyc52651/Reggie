Basic Info:

Spell Family: Jinx

Spell Alignment: Alignment

Silencing Spell, Personal (Silencio Locus)

Created by Evan Rosier and presented during his gift ceremony.

Effect: Makes all sounds created by the body silent while allowing the voice to be heard. As opposed to the Silencing Spell, General, which leaves footsteps, etc. audible.
