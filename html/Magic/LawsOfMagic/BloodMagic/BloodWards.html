Blood Wards

A type of protective barrier that must be unlocked using blood of a certain type

Known Blood Wards:

  • Blood ward in the Grimmauld Place Kitchen
    • Conceals a knife (SoS 161)
  • Blood ward to the Lestrange Manor dungeons
    • Mentioned in: SoS 161
    • Sirius opened this ward for the Aurors, claiming that it was keyed to Bellatrix (SoS 161)
    • The ward was actually keyed to Regulus' blood (SoS 161)
